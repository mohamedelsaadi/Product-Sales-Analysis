Just completed my latest Power BI project: Product Sales Analysis! 

I’m excited to share insights from this project that focuses on a detailed analysis of product sales across various regions.
- In this project, I’ve designed a Star Schema to optimize the data model, ensuring efficient analysis and reporting. The schema includes:
* Fact Table: Sales, containing metrics like sales, profit, units sold, and orders.
*Dimension Tables: Covering Products, Customers, Time, Regions, and Locations to allow for in-depth analysis across multiple perspectives.

 Key Highlights:

* Total Sales: $89.99
- Represents the sum of revenue generated from all product sales during the specified period. This metric helps in understanding the overall revenue performance of the business.

* Total Profit: $33.21
- Indicates the net earnings after subtracting costs from total sales. It shows how profitable the products are and helps in evaluating cost management and profitability.

* Units Sold: 2M
- Refers to the total quantity of products sold. This metric is crucial for gauging sales volume and understanding product demand.

* Total Orders: 9,644
The number of individual transactions or purchases made. It helps in analyzing customer buying patterns and transaction frequency.

* Regional Sales Contribution
- This insight breaks down total sales by region, helping identify high and low-performing regions. It provides a clear understanding of geographic sales performance and helps target marketing efforts effectively.

* Product Sales by Month
- Tracks monthly sales data to identify trends, seasonality, or fluctuations in product demand. This insight helps in strategic planning and inventory management.

* Top 10 Locations
- Lists the top 10 locations (could be cities or regions) based on total sales or units sold. This helps focus on high-value areas and optimize sales efforts in key markets.

* Total Sales by State
- This insight provides a state-wise breakdown of total sales, helping identify which states contribute the most to overall sales. It aids in regional strategy formulation and resource allocation.

* Sales by Quarter & Region
- Combines both time (quarterly) and region-specific sales data. This helps analyze regional performance over time, giving insights into how each region performs during different parts of the year.
